LONGEVITY & AESTHETICS
Built to last · Built to carry · Built to look the part
🫀 Cardiovascular Longevity
Your Zone 2 cycling base is one of the best longevity investments you can make. Studies on centenarians show sustained Zone 2 aerobic capacity is the #1 predictor of long-term health. Keep 60–70% of your cardio in Zone 2 (117–136 bpm for you). Your tennis spikes to Zone 5 naturally — this combination of base + peaks is optimal.
💪 Carry People Strength
To carry people, you need relative strength: deadlift 1.5–2× bodyweight (121–162kg for you at 81kg), strong posterior chain, grip strength, and core stability. Your deadlift, Bulgarian split squats, and bent-over rows are the right foundation. Track grip strength — it's one of the most reliable longevity biomarkers per research.
🧬 Longevity Protocols
At 26, your longevity investments compound. Prioritise: VO2 Max (you're building this), muscle mass preservation (strength training), sleep quality (7–9hrs), Zone 2 cardio, and mobility work. Cold exposure post-training, sauna 3×/week (if available), and consistent sleep schedule are evidence-backed longevity accelerators.
🥗 Nutrition for Longevity
For your goals: ~2.2g protein/kg bodyweight (≈178g/day). Prioritise whole foods, omega-3s (fatty fish 3×/week), polyphenols (berries, dark chocolate), and time-restricted eating (10–12hr eating window). Pre-workout: complex carbs 2hrs before. Post-workout: 30–40g protein within 45 min of lifting.
🏃 Endurance Beast Protocol
80/20 rule: 80% of your running in Zone 2, 20% at Zone 4–5. Your tennis naturally provides the 20%. Add one weekly long run (building to 15–20km at Zone 2 pace) once Phase 2 starts. VO2 Max above 50 ml/kg/min is the target — currently estimated ~42–46 based on your run data.
⚡ Body Aesthetics + Performance
At 188cm/81kg you have ideal frame for a lean athletic build. Target: 78–82kg at 10–13% body fat. This means muscle gain + fat loss simultaneously (body recomp) — achievable because you're intermediate. Keys: hit protein targets, stay in slight caloric deficit (200–300 kcal), prioritise compound lifts, and keep tennis for calorie burn.
